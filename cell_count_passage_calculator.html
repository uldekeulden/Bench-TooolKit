01 / Cell Counting

From count to concentration.

For a Neubauer hemocytometer, average the count per large square, multiply by 10⁴, then correct for any sample dilution (e.g. Trypan Blue).

Count input

Live
Enter the total count across the large squares you counted. Boundary rule: include cells touching two chosen outer edges (e.g. top/left) and exclude the opposite two.
Enter the total dilution factor directly: 2 for a two-fold dilution, 20 for a 20-fold dilution, etc.
1:1 cell sample : Trypan Blue means equal volumes and a 2× dilution. Example: 10 µL cells + 10 µL Trypan Blue.
cells/mL = (n ÷ number of large squares) × 10⁴ × dilution factor
ratio dilution factor = (sample parts + diluent parts) ÷ sample parts
total cells = cells/mL × volume (mL)

03 / Growth Rate

Infer growth from a real passage.

04 / Reference

Culture-vessel area reference.

Surface areas and approximate growth-medium volumes are drawn from Thermo Fisher's “Useful Numbers for Cell Culture,” except the ibidi 8-well chamber (manufacturer-specified area). Plate/chamber areas are per well.

Important: confluency-to-cell-number relationships vary substantially by cell line, morphology, vessel coating, attachment efficiency, and handling. The confluency model is a planning estimate based on relative occupied surface area and the growth multiplier you provide; it does not assume Thermo Fisher's HeLa “cells at confluency” values.

Bench planning aid · not a substitute for cell-line-specific SOPs or empirical growth curves.
